WHAT WE DO. AND WE DO IT REALLY WELL.

We are a small, senior studio that designs, builds and runs software for teams who need it working in production rather than described in a deck. Pick a single service or hand us the whole delivery.

WHAT WE OFFER

  • Custom Software

    Product and platform work built to your domain rather than bent out of an off-the-shelf tool, from the first release through to the version your customers depend on.

    • TypeScript
    • Go
    • Python
    • PostgreSQL

  • Team Augmentation

    Senior engineers who join your standups, your repository and your review queue, and who leave your team faster than they found it rather than dependent on ours.

    • Embedded squads
    • Code review
    • Mentoring

  • Cloud & Infrastructure

    Environments described in code, deployed on a pipeline anyone on the team can run, and sized so the bill matches the traffic instead of the worst week of the year.

    • AWS
    • Kubernetes
    • Terraform
    • CI/CD

  • AI & Automation

    Assistants, extraction and routing wired into the systems you already run, with evaluations in place so you can tell an improvement from a regression before your customers do.

    • LLM integration
    • Retrieval
    • Evaluation harnesses

  • Data Engineering

    Pipelines, warehouses and contracts that turn scattered operational tables into numbers your analysts and your board can both agree on.

    • dbt
    • Airflow
    • Snowflake
    • Streaming

  • Project Governance

    Delivery oversight for work already underway: scope held steady, risks surfaced early, and a weekly read on progress that survives contact with the schedule.

    • Discovery
    • Roadmapping
    • Delivery reviews

  • Rapid Proof of Concept

    A working, throwaway-if-need-be build in weeks rather than quarters, so the decision to invest is taken against something you can click rather than something you can only imagine.

    • Prototyping
    • Spike work
    • Feasibility

  • UI/UX Design

    Interface and interaction design done next to the engineers building it, shipped as a component library your team can extend instead of a set of screens they have to interpret.

    • Design systems
    • Prototypes
    • Accessibility
